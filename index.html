Wind Actions to AS/NZS 1170.2:2021 basic calculator

Site wind speed, design wind speed and preliminary design wind pressure for enclosed rectangular buildings.

Scope & disclaimer: This is a preliminary/educational tool, not a substitute for the full Standard or engineering judgement. It covers Sections 2–4 (site & design wind speed) and a simplified Section 5 (enclosed rectangular buildings, walls and roof pitch <10° only). It does not cover: Section 6 dynamic response (Cdyn is left as a manual input, default 1.0), Appendices A–E, area reduction/combination/local pressure/porous cladding factors (Ka, Kc, Kl, Kp are taken as 1.0), NZ lee-zone multipliers, C/D coastal-distance interpolation, or pitched roofs ≥10°. Always verify against AS/NZS 1170.2:2021 and have results checked by a suitably qualified engineer. Full scope notes →

Walls are fully covered (Tables 5.2(A)–(C)). Roofs are only covered for pitch <10° (Table 5.3(A)) — for pitched roofs ≥10°, enter a Cp,e value you've sourced from Tables 5.3(B)/(C) directly. Ka, Kc, Kl, Kp are all taken as 1.0 (no area reduction / local cladding factors) — apply these separately for cladding and fixing design.
